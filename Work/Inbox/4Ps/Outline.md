
**Context**  
- [https://coda.io/@logangreen/lyft-3p-ritual](https://coda.io/@logangreen/lyft-3p-ritual)

  
**Objectives**  

- Make is easier for the team to understand what matters most and what actions are being taken
- Bring clarity to the priorities required to deliver on the OKRs and user problems being focused on
- Reflect on the progress that has been made on delivering short term plans and longer term priorities
- Provide insight into what might be coming up and any challenges that might be impacting your ability to deliver
- Create an opportunity to clarify and provide feedback

  
**Framework**  

Priorities
- What matters most right now.
- 2–3 outcome areas linked to OKRs or key user problems.
- These usually don’t change week to week.

Progress.
- What we achieved or learned last week
- Follow up on the plan from last week to maintain consistency
- Were the plans achieved, did they change?
- Wins, milestones, or learnings.

Plans.
- What’s coming next
- The major actions or deliverables for the week ahead that drive your priorities.
- These will become next week's progress updates

Problems.
- What is blocking the team
- Challenges or blockers slowing progress.
- Where you need support or decisions.
