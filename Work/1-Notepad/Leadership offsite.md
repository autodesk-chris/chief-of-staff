Recap of the AI Challenge at the Growth and Adoption offsite

Our group included marketing, Product adoption, Design, PMs and Engineers. Most people used ChatGPT, but not the tools startups are using to move fast - Claude Code and others.

We used a Claude Code expert and a founder of a company building agents for non-technical people as mentors

We challenged the team to embrace AI to move faster than they thought possible. Everyone was introduced to Claude Code, Github and other tools. Everyone started by vibecoding a website for the Building Design launch. They did it in about 45 mins. 

Then we moved onto a challenge. They had to build a productivity tool or a prototype. Every was expected to demo a working agent or feature. And they had less than 4 hours to do it.
