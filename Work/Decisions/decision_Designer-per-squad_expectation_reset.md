# Decision: Designer-per-squad expectation reset

## Decision

One designer per squad is no longer realistic at current headcount. All designers treated as generalists; some squads have no designer assigned.

## Context

The current operating model assumed one designer per squad. That expectation creates friction because the 1:1 ratio never fully existed and headcount cannot stretch to cover every squad.

Initiatives currently with no designer assigned: Unify Project, ACC Convergence, Ecosystem, Compass (until later), Adaptive Trust, Embedded AI, Land Development, Building Automation.

Mitigations discussed:
- Borrow from XF (limited, other orgs have worse ratios)
- Add consultants (requires Carl approval, currently no)
- Connect squads without designers to relevant XF contacts as design liaisons
- Explore with Christina: committed ratios for XF support to this org

## Communications plan

- Sonia owns the comms: quick Slack post now, fuller explanation at All Hands June 20
- Story to be built and vetted with Carl before committing to the slot
- Communication must be empathetic: the 1:1 ratio never fully existed anyway
- Preference to fold into Carl's broader "way of the land" messaging rather than a standalone section

Sonia (comms), Carl (final messaging frame)
