## Areas for Improvement

**Scaling Impact Through Delegation and Scalable Solutions**

Joseph Price identifies a critical challenge: "Anders should be mindful of taking on too much himself and double down on looking for scalable options to handle requests from account teams either by delegating within the squad (or Forma Design) or even sometimes say no and deflect to self serve methods." He notes that "Strategic Accounts and Anders could be even more successful if they can rebalance the amount of direct engagements and make time to prioritize developing scaleable strategies." This pattern of defaulting to direct engagement over scalable solutions limits the squad's potential impact and puts unsustainable pressure on Anders. Ben Storey reinforces this concern from a different angle, suggesting opportunities for Anders to "move forward independently in certain areas, especially where existing resources or assets are already in place" rather than waiting for cross-functional partners. Developing stronger delegation skills and actively seeking leverage points would allow Anders to multiply his impact while creating growth opportunities for team members.

**Transparent Communication and Strategic Visibility**

Multiple team members identify significant gaps in communication and transparency. Sid Arora expresses a clear need: "More visibility into broader decisions across the Forma organization—both management and cross-squad updates—would help us stay better connected to the wider context and improve alignment, especially given our time-zone challenges and the different priorities and situations across markets." Robin Franke echoes this more pointedly: "There are many things he works on with leadership that I feel I'm not aware of and find out about after the fact. It would be great to better understand the responsibilities he's doing as a manager like strategy and formation of new teams. In some ways it feels like my geo excludes me being part of the conversation and the lack of transparency plays into this." These gaps in strategic communication create feelings of exclusion and reduce team alignment, particularly challenging for a geographically distributed squad. As a squad lead, Anders needs to establish more predictable communication rhythms that keep the team informed of strategic decisions, organizational changes, and cross-functional developments.

**People Development and Career Conversations**

Robin Franke identifies a significant gap in people management: "In the past, I had conversations with my manager on professional development and what I could do to develop myself based on the career goals I had. I have not had that conversation with Anders since he has taken the role of manager... In general, the lack of career progression on our team is very unclear and does not make me secure in my role. I have expressed this to Anders many times and I haven't heard any progress on addressing this." This represents a fundamental people management responsibility that isn't being met. Sid Arora expands on this need: "Discussing and sharing more feedback, expertise, and insights on actual initiatives could help the team grow and learn continuously. We currently operate well at a high strategic level, but structured knowledge sharing and guidance would complement his operational focus, would increase collaboration within the team, and help us work more cohesively." As a relatively new manager, Anders would benefit from foundational management training—Joseph Price specifically recommends "the 'manager essentials' course which provides some foundational theory and people management as well as being part of a cohort to share and discuss experiences."

**Technical and Platform Depth**

Michael Juros identifies a knowledge gap that could limit Anders's effectiveness with increasingly sophisticated customers: "Anders will need to know more about the overall platform, APIs, and connection/integration possibilities with the many 3rd party Apps out there." As enterprise customers mature in their Forma adoption, they increasingly require guidance on integrations, extensibility, and technical architecture. Developing deeper platform knowledge would strengthen Anders's ability to have strategic technical conversations and position Forma's enterprise capabilities effectively.
