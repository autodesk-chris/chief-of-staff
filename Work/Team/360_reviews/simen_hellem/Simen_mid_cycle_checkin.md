

  

## 1. Data Literacy & Self-Serve

- Lead the weekly data forum with high engagement, clear narratives, and consistency — so it becomes a must-have ritual. 
- Ensure the product team understands core metrics (activation, retention, engagement, MAU) — not just numbers, but what drives them. 
- Drive Mixpanel proficiency: PMs and designers should be able to self-serve funnels, cohorts, and retention analyses. 
- Extend data fluency to the leadership team through a monthly “metrics pulse” report with clear storytelling.

👉 Success = 70–80% of routine product data questions answered without analyst intervention. 

---

## 2. Experimentation Excellence

- Establish a playbook for A/B vs. multivariate (ABCD) tests: when to use, trade-offs, guardrails. 
- Uphold a high bar of rigor: ensure tests are powered, guardrails defined, and interpretation is consistent. 
- Educate PMs and designers on common pitfalls (false positives, peeking, sample size issues).

👉 Success = tests consistently trusted, faster experimentation velocity without loss of rigor. 

---

## 3. MAU Growth Model & Planning

- Own the MAU growth plan: build transparent models showing how acquisition, activation, retention, and expansion add up to targets. 
- Provide scenario modeling so leadership understands trade-offs (e.g., “+5% retention = +X MAUs”). 
- Ensure PMs link their initiatives to specific MAU levers.

👉 Success = MAU model is a reference point in strategy discussions; PMs talk about levers, not just features. 

---

## 4. Proactive Insights

- Move from reactive reporting to insight generation: deliver regular “insight packs” that highlight opportunities and risks without being asked. 
- Ensure learnings from experiments are captured, codified, and shared. 
- Spot anomalies and trends before they’re raised by others.

👉 Success = PMs and leadership can point to specific product/growth decisions sparked by data team insights. 

---

## 5. Qual + Quant Integration

- Build processes to connect Intercom, CS feedback, and other qualitative sources to product usage data. 
- Deliver quantified stories: “X% of churners cite Y (qual), which maps to Z behavior drop-off (quant).” 
- Partner with PMs/UX to embed “why” alongside “what.”

👉 Success = leadership conversations routinely include both quant trends and the customer voice. 

---

## 6. Strategic Leadership & Enablement

- Roadmap: Maintain a clear maturity plan (data quality → self-serve → warehouse leverage → predictive/advanced). 
- Enablement over bottlenecking: train, document, and empower others to answer questions independently. 
- Alignment: Act as bridge with corporate data/IT to leverage central assets without duplication.

👉 Success = org is steadily more confident, self-sufficient, and forward-looking in data use. 

---

## 7. Team Leadership

- Develop your 2 ICs so they can handle the bulk of analysis and dashboarding, freeing you for strategic focus. 
- Maintain a high bar for analysis quality: consistent frameworks, clear write-ups, actionable recommendations. 
- Build a culture of curiosity and evidence across the org.

👉 Success = team scales impact without scaling headcount linearly; PMs feel well supported.
