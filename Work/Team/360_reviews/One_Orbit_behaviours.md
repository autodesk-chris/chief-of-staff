

Below is a **performance-oriented interpretation** of each One ORBIT dimension for assessment purposes.

---

### ONE AUTODESK – _Fast or far, we go together_

**How to assess:**

- Collaborates across teams and functions
    
- Prioritizes shared goals over individual wins
    
- Builds inclusive environments where others feel heard
    

**Signals of strong performance:**

- Breaks silos to solve problems
    
- Actively includes relevant stakeholders
    
- Makes decisions that benefit the broader organization
    

---

### OPTIMISTIC – _We believe in better_

**How to assess:**

- Maintains a solutions-focused mindset
    
- Responds constructively to feedback and change
    
- Contributes to a learning and growth culture
    

**Signals of strong performance:**

- Provides timely, actionable feedback
    
- Approaches challenges with curiosity rather than defensiveness
    
- Invests in continuous learning and improvement
    

---

### RELENTLESS – _We’re unstoppable in the pursuit of results_

**How to assess:**

- Demonstrates accountability and urgency
    
- Prioritizes effectively and adapts as needed
    
- Follows through despite obstacles
    

**Signals of strong performance:**

- Adjusts course when new information emerges
    
- Anticipates risks and opportunities
    
- Balances speed with thoughtful execution
    

---

### BRAVE – _We take smart risks and make bold decisions_

**How to assess:**

- Takes informed risks supported by data and insight
    
- Speaks up and engages in difficult conversations
    
- Stands behind decisions once made
    

**Signals of strong performance:**

- Challenges the status quo when needed
    
- Addresses conflict directly and constructively
    
- Takes accountability even when outcomes are not ideal
    

---

### INGENIOUS – _We reimagine what is possible_

**How to assess:**

- Applies creativity and experimentation
    
- Solves problems resourcefully within constraints
    
- Seeks continuous improvement
    

**Signals of strong performance:**

- Tests ideas and learns quickly
    
- Introduces new or improved approaches
    
- Eliminates work that no longer adds value
    

---

### TRUSTED – _We earn and build trust through every interaction_

**How to assess:**

- Demonstrates integrity, reliability, and empathy
    
- Communicates transparently
    
- Produces consistent, high-quality work
    

**Signals of strong performance:**

- Delivers on commitments
    
- Owns mistakes and course-corrects
    
- Builds strong, trust-based relationships
