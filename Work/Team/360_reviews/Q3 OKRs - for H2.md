
**Objective:**  
Embed a product-led mindset through high-quality, high-velocity experimentation  
Key Results:  

1. We measure and track experiment velocity on a weekly basis
2. All experiments or initiatives started after 01 July are added to the Experiment Airtable, follow the relevant brief template and pass a bar-raiser or LLM quality check based on our defined best practices.
3. Weekly experiment review sessions take place and the outcomes shared with the Product Area
4. Key artifacts including Squad strategy and worldviews are up to date and sharable
5. 100% of squads maintain and share metric observations, questions, and hypotheses linked to their focus area bi-weekly

  
**Objective: Increase New User acquisition**  
Architects with early-stage design, or analysis needs, and also use Revit or Docs, log in and create or join a project.  
Key results  

1. 2000 new MAU users per month by the end of July 2025 and 3000 waitlist signups for new capabilities (by end of FY26)
2. The percentage of New MAUs getting into a site in their first 7 days is 80% by Jul 2025. (increased from 74% in Jan 2025)
3. The bounce rate of users not reaching a hub in their first session is less than 20% by Jul 2025 (from 25%)
4. Increase number of new users joining from invitations from X in Jan to Y in September (+Z%).

  
**Objective****: Improve New User Retention Rate (NURR):**  
  
A NEW user gets value from using our most important functionality quickly.  
A NEW user completes project setup workflow, runs an analysis, creates a building, uses an extension or uses board in their first 7 days.  
Key results  

1. 50% of new users complete the 'project setup workflow' **(WF1)** OR runs two analysis **(Activation)** OR creates a building **(WF2)** OR uses an extension (**Revit or 3rd party)** OR uses board in their first 7 days (improved from 43% in Jan 2025).
2. 40% of new users complete the 'design setup workflow' (**WF1**) in their first 7 days (improved from 10% at end Jan 2025)
3. 25% of new users do two analyses (**Activation**) in their first 7 days (from 19% in Jan 2025)
4. 35% of new users use an extension (**Revit or a 3rd party extension**) in their first 7 days. Baseline usage is 28%)
5. 8% of new users use **Board** in their first 7 days (from 4%)

  
**Objective: Improve Current User Retention Rate (CURR):**  
A Current user does a meaningful* action on 2+ separate days in the previous 30-days  
Key results  

1. 45% of MAUs are engaged min 2 distinct days in the last 30 days (baseline 35%)

  
_Meaningful actions: (1) Create project + order data +add site limit - WF1, (2) Add buildings - WF2, (3) Compare analysis - WF3, (4) Save a screenshot - WF4, (5) Do two analyses - Activation, (6) Use Revit, (7) Use an extension, (7) Send an invitation, (9) Use board._
