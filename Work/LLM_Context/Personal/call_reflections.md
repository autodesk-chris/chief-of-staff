## 28 April 2026 - Simon coaching plan Monday check-in

**Patterns observed:**

**Over-explaining (Pattern 3):**
- Extended "busy hamster" metaphor explaining outcomes vs tasks (~30 seconds). Shorter: "What happens if you're busy all week but the numbers don't move?"

**Giving answers (Pattern 4):**
- Baseline explanation: "It doesn't have to be perfect, just consistent" - gave the answer. Better: "What would make this baseline useful week over week?"
- AI agents tutorial: Full explanation of what agents are, how to find them, when to use them. Simon invited it ("you might already have a few ideas"), but a coaching response would be: "Check the repository and come back with what you think fits your workflow."
- Priority ordering: "Number one is coaching plan, number two is AI, number three is team" - appropriate as a manager but also does his prioritisation for him.

**Airtime (Pattern 5):** Not formally measured, but several extended segments of Chris talking.

**What went well:**
- "Over to you" opening - let Simon lead
- "What output do you want to get from this?" - clean question
- "At the end of this week what does success look like?" - outcome-focused
- Creator agreement challenge via empathy: "How would you feel if I said sign this agreement?" - made Simon see it from the other side without telling him the answer
- "What are the sources that ensure you have the information needed to be successful?" - pushed ownership

**Progress vs previous call:** Significantly better than week 1. More questions, less direction. The creator agreement moment was a highlight. Main area to keep working on: length of explanations and resisting the pull when someone invites guidance.
