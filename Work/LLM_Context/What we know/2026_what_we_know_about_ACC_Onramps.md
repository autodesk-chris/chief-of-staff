

## Summary

Forma’s biggest growth wins in 2025 come from removing friction for high-intent users and adding contextual guidance for low-intent users, but no experiment thus far meaningfully converts low-intent ACC traffic into high-quality users.

ACC-email is a powerful acquisition channel in volume, but structurally weak in quality. We believe most users arriving via ACC-email are not Forma’s IUP, lack clear intent, and require significant context to engage.

We learned that ACC-email users behave as a distinct, lower-intent segment that benefits from contextual guidance but reacts poorly to abstract onboarding or intent questioning. While onboarding improvements and tutorials can meaningfully increase early actions, these gains do not translate into step-change engagement or sustained value. High-impact growth came from friction removal for already-motivated users, not from converting passive ACC traffic. This reinforces that the primary constraint is upstream audience quality and intent, not downstream experience optimization.
