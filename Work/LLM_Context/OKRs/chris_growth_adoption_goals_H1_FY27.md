
# Growth & Adoption goals - H1 FY27 (Feb - Sep 2026)

**Owner:** Chris Small
**Period:** H1 FY27 through AU (February - September 2026)
**Primary lens:** Win in the US market. UK is secondary but important - we believe winning in the US creates flow-on benefits for the UK.

**User focus:** Mid-career architects at small US firms (1-50 AEC-C seats), working on early-stage mixed-use residential. They're under pressure to move fast, often do early-stage work unpaid, and need to prove feasibility quickly. They're both user and admin - no gatekeeper blocking adoption.

Note: Metrics are estimates. Awaiting updates

**Why:** ~79% of new commercial MAUs never enter a site. Of those who do, only ~15-20% reach first strike. 70-85% of meaningful actions happen in the first session. The path from arrival to value needs to be seamless - profiling routes users to the right recipe, onboarding guides them through it.

**Accountability signals:** Day-1 FSM rate; profiling experiment lift; NURR for site-entered users (currently 46%, target 50%)

## O7: Build a high-performing AI-first team

**Why:** Our operating model - small squads leveraging AI - is a forcing function for better thinking. AI isn't just individual productivity; the real leverage is at the squad level when handoffs shrink and prototyping accelerates. Experiment velocity is our competitive advantage. If everything works, we're not being ambitious enough.

| #   | Key result                                                        | Baseline        | Target                                                                                                   | Owner           |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| KR1 | Experimentation velocity across squads with documented learnings  | Varies by squad | 16+ experiments/quarter across Product Development squads.                                               | Even & Maria    |
| KR2 | Every squad has a documented and active AI productivity strategy  | Partial         | All squads have strategies reviewed against the Forma Design framework, with measurable behaviour change | All squad leads |
| KR3 | Strong understanding of mutual expectations with leadership peers | None            | Documented understanding with Product leads & strategy                                                   | Chris           |

Carl asked all leadership team members to invest more in mutual expectations - understanding how each person wants others to show up, and being clear on accountability between roles. This prevents heated moments from derailing progress.

**Accountability signals:** 60-70% experiment failure rate (ambition indicator); cross-squad learning instances per month; time from idea to live experiment
