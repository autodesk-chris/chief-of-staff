# The destination

## Why we're doing this

Most users who try Forma don't come back. They get stuck, don't find value in their first session, and disappear. We lose them not because the product isn't good, but because they didn't get the guidance they needed at the moment they needed it.

User Engagement exists to change that equation. The mission is simple: every user should feel empowered to succeed. Not through human hand-holding that doesn't scale, but through AI-powered systems that provide personalized, timely support to every user - monitoring their journey, intervening when they're stuck, answering their questions instantly, and giving them the learning they need when they need it.

This is the future of user success. We're building what a world-class customer success organization looks like when you have AI agents doing the work. If we get this right, we'll be at the leading edge of user success in the AI era - and other teams across Autodesk and beyond will be seeking us out to learn how we did it.

## What success looks like

- **Every user feels supported**: Users feel like they're getting one-to-one attention even though AI is doing the work. They get timely interventions when stuck, instant answers to questions, and personalized learning when they need it
- **Information is helpful, not burdensome**: Content is easily digestible and appears when needed - supportive rather than onerous. Users have the information they want, when they want it
- **Leading edge of AI user success**: Other teams seek out our knowledge on how to build an effective user success function at scale. We're recognized as pioneers in using AI for customer success
- **Jobs to be done mapped with agents doing them**: We've identified the key user success jobs and have AI agents executing them systematically
- **Learning velocity**: Running multiple experiments each month to understand what works and why

## The focus

- **One-to-one at scale through AI**: Trig and other AI systems monitoring every user's journey and intervening at the right moment with the right message
- **Real-time support via agents**: FIN and Autodesk Assistant ensuring every user gets answers fast - no waiting, no friction
- **Personalized learning on demand**: Users access training when they need it, in the format that works for them - not forced through generic courses
- **Deep user understanding**: Qualitative interviews to understand gaps, challenges, and why experiments work or don't

## How I expect you to work

**Drive strategy proactively**: I want to work closely on defining strategy, but I'm looking for you to be proactive and drive strategy more and more. The ideal state is you driving me rather than the other way around. Don't wait for direction - propose it.

**Move fast, apologize later**: Adopt more of an "apologize rather than ask permission" approach. You've been cautious to avoid appearing pushy - but boldness is what's needed. Speed matters. Make the call, run the experiment, learn.

**Experiment constantly**: Run multiple experiments each month. Not everything will work - that's the point. Build a systematic approach to testing interventions and understanding what drives user success.

**Own the Help Center**: Take ownership of the Help Center and its evolution. Work closely with Daria on content strategy to ensure help content is high-quality, up-to-date, and integrated with the broader support experience.

**Build organizational visibility**: Share what you're learning. The user success work is pioneering - make sure leadership and other teams see it. Build the narrative around what you're building.

# Detailed expectations

### Scale one-to-one user engagement through Trig

**What**: Use Trig to automatically detect where each user is in their journey and deliver timely, contextually relevant interventions.

**Key activities**:
- Build and maintain the 7-stage lifecycle in Trig (Set Up, First Strike, Exploration, Retention stages)
- Create rescue jobs that intervene when users fall off the path
- Implement ICP cohort targeting to prioritize the right users
- Complete Salesforce integration to enable account activity write-back for sales team
- Track lifecycle stage progression linked to Trig-sent messages

**Expectations**: The goal is that every new user feels personally taken care of - receiving messages at the right moment, relevant to exactly where they are in their journey, without anyone manually monitoring. Trig acts as a virtual CS team for every account. Measure FSM rate days 2-7 (currently 2.8%, target 10%) and NURR improvement.

### Deliver real-time support through AI agents

**What**: Ensure every user gets instant answers through FIN, Autodesk Assistant, and integrated support systems.

**Key activities**:
- Operate and optimize FIN as the primary in-product support agent
- Participate in Autodesk Assistant taskforce and integration planning
- Work toward shared support model across Forma Design and Construction
- Coordinate with CTS (Customer Technical Support) on case routing and escalation
- Maintain high FIN resolution rate (currently 89%, target 85%+)

**Expectations**: Users should get answers fast - measured in seconds, not hours. The support experience should feel seamless regardless of which agent or channel they use. As Autodesk Assistant launches (April FY27), ensure smooth transition while maintaining service quality. The decision to move toward a shared support model means coordinating closely with CTS and ensuring Forma's needs are represented.

### Own the Help Center and content strategy

**What**: Take ownership of the Help Center experience and ensure help content supports user success.

**Key activities**:
- Own the Help Center experience and evolution
- Work closely with Daria on content strategy and content quality
- Migrate help and learning resources to ADSK Help/Learn
- Ensure top monthly FSM blockers have clear help content created within 30 days
- Coordinate with Content Strategy on coherent overall content approach

**Expectations**: Help content should be a strategic asset, not an afterthought. Content should appear when users need it, be easily digestible, and actually help users succeed. The migration to help.autodesk.com is essential for Forma Industry Cloud consolidation - drive this with urgency.

### Build personalized learning at scale

**What**: Ensure users can access training when they need it, in the format that works for them.

**Key activities**:
- Provide access to third-party learning courses
- Develop learning paths for Building Design (launching April 2026)
- Coordinate with Autodesk Learn on content updates and localization
- Track learning content consumption and correlation with user success
- Build self-serve resources that users actually use

**Expectations**: Currently only 9% of new users consume learning content in first 30 days - but those who do are 3x more likely to take meaningful action. Find ways to get more users to the right learning content at the right time. Learning should feel personalized and helpful, not like mandatory training.

### Generate deep user insights through research

**What**: Build systematic user research that informs product decisions and explains experiment results.

**Key activities**:
- Conduct 10 user interviews per month
- Share top monthly FSM insights with PMs, with mitigations included in sprint/experiment plans
- Explain why experiments work or don't through qualitative understanding
- Feed user insights into product roadmap decisions
- Build relationships with user research teams (collaborate with Lisa on ECC perspective)

**Expectations**: Quantitative data tells you what's happening; qualitative research tells you why. Every major experiment should have a qualitative component that helps us understand the result. User insights should be a continuous input to product decisions, not an occasional report.

### Run a high-velocity experimentation engine

**What**: Continuously test interventions and approaches to understand what drives user success.

**Key activities**:
- Run multiple experiments per month across engagement, learning, and support
- Document learnings from each experiment (wins and failures)
- Build on what works (like single-CTA approach: 10% vs 2% conversion)
- Investigate unexpected results (survey emails boosting board completion 23%)
- Share learnings across Growth team

**Expectations**: Not everything will work. The single-job email approach achieving 10% conversion vs 2% for multi-step shows that precision matters. Keep experimenting to find what works. Document and share learnings so we build organizational knowledge.

### Build the user success strategy

**What**: Develop and maintain a comprehensive user success at scale strategy.

**Key activities**:
- Own the User Engagement strategy document and keep it current
- Develop 12-month strategic vision for user success at scale
- Identify the jobs to be done and map agents to each job
- Build the case for user engagement as a strategic capability
- Share strategy broadly to build organizational visibility

**Expectations**: Move from OKR structuring to proactive strategy work. The strategy should articulate what user success looks like at scale, how AI enables it, and why it matters. This is pioneering work - make sure it's visible.

# Operating principles

1. **Every user empowered to succeed**: This is the mission. Every decision should be evaluated against whether it helps users succeed.

2. **One-to-one at scale**: The goal is personal attention for every user, delivered through AI. If it requires manual intervention, it doesn't scale.

3. **Speed over perfection**: Move fast, experiment, learn. Apologize rather than ask permission. Boldness beats caution.

4. **Jobs to be done, agents doing them**: Identify the user success jobs, then build or configure agents to execute them systematically.

5. **Insights drive action**: User research isn't a report - it's an input to product decisions and experiment design. Every insight should lead to action.

6. **Build for the future**: We're building what AI-powered user success looks like. Think long-term, share what we learn, become the example others follow.
