## First Strike

- **Lead:** Even Olstad
- **Slack user ID:** U0214U4F84X
- **Slack channel:** #forma-growth-squad-first-strike (C08B45K9SU8)
- **MFM owner:** Even Olstad
- **OKR DRI:** Even Olstad (7-day return rate), Maria Chefneux (monetization)
- **Mission:** Drive users to experience enough value in their first session to return. The most direct lever for retention and sustainable MAU growth.
- **Key focus areas:**
  - High-velocity experimentation engine (16+ experiments/quarter)
  - Root cause analysis of drop-off, not just funnel optimization
  - Fastest path from entry to first strike for Site Design and Building Design
  - Reusable onboarding and activation patterns across apps
