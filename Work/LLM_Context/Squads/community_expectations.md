# The destination

## Why we're doing this

We have thousands of users getting value from Forma. But most of that value stays invisible - users don't share what they're doing, don't tell others about the product, don't represent us in the conversations that matter. Meanwhile, our competitors have louder voices despite having less to say.

Community exists to change that equation. The goal isn't just to "build a community" - it's to turn our users into a powerful voice for Forma that we couldn't create ourselves. When architects talk about early-stage design, when they share work online, when they show up at events - we want Forma and outcome-based design to be part of that conversation. Not because we're paying for ads, but because our users are genuinely talking about us.

This is a pillar of the Marketing strategy, not a separate initiative. Community, Content, and Product Center work together to own share of voice around outcome-based design. Community's specific role is activating users as voices - turning the people who already love Forma into amplifiers of our message.

## What success looks like

- **Forma is everywhere**: If you're an architect and you open LinkedIn, you see something about Forma. Leadership (Carl, Amy) are delighted by the volume and quality of content
- **Clear positioning in the market**: Potential users understand that Forma is the leading tool for early-stage design. The outcome-based design message comes through in community content
- **Scalable content engines running**: A design competition generating ongoing content. An insiders programme with engaged advocates. Other assets that continuously produce user-generated content without heavy manual effort
- **Users as voices**: Community members are representing Forma at events, in forums, online - telling their stories, not ours

## The focus

- **Activate users, don't create content for them**: The goal is users generating and sharing content. Community enables and amplifies, not produces
- **Build scalable assets**: One-off campaigns don't compound. Invest in programmes (competitions, insiders, etc.) that continuously generate content
- **Outcome-based design messaging**: Everything reinforces the narrative. Users should be talking about comparing options and achieving outcomes, not just "cool software"
- **Experiment and challenge**: Create an online personality that challenges the status quo. Don't be afraid to be a little controversial. Safe and boring doesn't cut through

## How I expect you to work

**Pillar of Marketing**: Community strategy aligns tightly with Marketing. You're not operating independently - you're one of the core channels for owning share of voice. Work closely with Marketing on messaging, initiatives, and priorities.

**Build scalable engines**: Don't get stuck doing things manually that should be systematised. The design competition, insiders programme, and other assets should become machines that produce content with decreasing marginal effort.

**Experiment actively**: Try things. Some will fail. That's fine. We're building a new function and need to learn what works. Don't wait for permission to test ideas.

**Create a personality**: The Forma community presence should have character. Challenge conventional thinking about design tools. Be opinionated about outcome-based design. A little controversy is fine - bland doesn't break through.

**Leverage Marketing initiatives**: A lot of community activity should build on what Marketing is already doing - events, content, product launches. Don't reinvent when you can amplify.

## My role

- Provide strategic context on how Community fits with overall Growth and Marketing strategy
- Help navigate stakeholder relationships (Carl, Amy, Product teams)
- Clear blockers on budget, hiring, or cross-functional issues
- Challenge whether we're building for scale vs. doing things manually
- Provide air cover for bold experiments

# Detailed expectations

### Turn users into content creators and advocates

**What**: Activate Forma users to share their work, tell their stories, and represent us in conversations online and offline.

**Key activities**:
- Identify and engage power users who could become advocates
- Create easy paths for users to share their Forma work
- Equip advocates with messaging and assets that reinforce outcome-based design
- Build relationships with users who show up at events and forums

**Expectations**: The metric isn't how much content we create - it's how much content users create. Every programme should be evaluated on whether it's activating user voices at scale.

### Build the insiders programme

**What**: Create a structured programme for engaged advocates who represent Forma in the community.

**Key activities**:
- Define the insiders programme structure, benefits, and expectations
- Recruit initial cohort of engaged users
- Equip insiders with content, messaging, and early access
- Track insider activity and impact on share of voice

**Expectations**: Insiders should feel like genuine advocates, not paid shills. The programme gives them value (access, recognition, community) in exchange for authentic engagement. Build for quality of advocates, not just quantity.

### Launch and scale a design competition

**What**: Create a recurring design competition that generates ongoing content and engagement.

**Key activities**:
- Design competition format, cadence, and judging criteria
- Build partnerships (judges, sponsors, media partners) that extend reach
- Create content pipeline from competition entries and winners
- Systematise so each iteration requires less manual effort

**Expectations**: The competition should become a content engine - entries generate shareable work, winners become case studies, the event itself drives engagement. Each round should be more efficient than the last.

### Build other scalable content assets

**What**: Create additional programmes and assets that continuously generate user content.

**Key activities**:
- Identify opportunities for scalable content generation
- Test and iterate on formats that work
- Build systems that reduce manual effort over time
- Measure content output and reach per unit of effort

**Expectations**: Think like a platform, not a content team. What can we build once that produces content continuously? Templates, challenges, showcases, user spotlights - find formats that scale.

### Own community presence and personality

**What**: Develop a distinctive Forma community voice that cuts through the noise.

**Key activities**:
- Define the community personality and tone
- Create content that challenges status quo thinking about design tools
- Engage in conversations with a point of view, not just promotion
- Be willing to be a little controversial when it reinforces our positioning

**Expectations**: Safe and corporate doesn't work for community. We need personality. The community presence should feel like a person with opinions, not a brand account. Push boundaries - better to be too bold and dial back than to be forgettable.

### Drive LinkedIn and social presence

**What**: Make Forma ubiquitous on LinkedIn and relevant social channels for architects.

**Key activities**:
- Build consistent posting cadence with community-generated content
- Engage with architect conversations and communities
- Amplify user content and advocate voices
- Track reach and engagement metrics (40k→500k/mo LinkedIn reach target)

**Expectations**: The goal is that architects can't open LinkedIn without seeing something Forma-related. This isn't just our posts - it's the community talking about us. Measure both our reach and the broader conversation.

### Activate community at events

**What**: Enable community members to represent Forma at industry events and create their own.

**Key activities**:
- Identify key events where architects gather and activate community attendance
- Provide assets, materials, and support for community members attending events
- Enable and support community members to create and run their own Forma events
- Provide budget support for community-led events where appropriate
- Create content from event presence and community-run events

**Expectations**: We can't be everywhere, but our community can. The goal is community members showing up at events as advocates and running their own local gatherings. Equip them with what they need - assets, messaging, and sometimes budget - to represent us effectively.

### Align closely with Marketing

**What**: Ensure Community operates as a pillar of Marketing strategy, not a separate initiative.

**Key activities**:
- Regular alignment with Marketing on priorities and messaging
- Leverage Marketing initiatives for community activation
- Coordinate content calendars and campaign timing
- Share learnings and insights across teams

**Expectations**: Community and Marketing should feel like one team with different focus areas. If there's daylight between Community messaging and Marketing messaging, something is wrong. Stay tight.

# Operating principles

1. **Users as voices**: Our job is to activate users, not speak for them. The most credible content is what users create and share themselves.

2. **Build for scale**: Every programme should get more efficient over time. If we're doing the same manual work in month 6 as month 1, we're doing it wrong.

3. **Personality over polish**: The community presence should have character. Opinionated, challenging, a little controversial. Bland doesn't break through.

4. **Experiment actively**: We're building something new. Try things, learn fast, double down on what works. Don't wait for certainty before acting.

5. **Pillar of Marketing**: Community isn't separate - it's one of the core channels for owning share of voice. Stay tightly aligned with Marketing strategy and messaging.

6. **Outcome-based design always**: Every piece of community content should reinforce the narrative. Users comparing options to achieve outcomes. That's what we're about.
