

### Overview

**Purpose**

Provide space for squads and leadership to examine progress and decide what to do next.

**Desired Outcome**

Clear monthly focus, confidence in quarterly targets, and early identification of issues.

**Agenda**

- **Metrics:** Review the key metrics for strategy units and squads; surface issues.
- **Review:** Reflect on the previous month’s focus areas and bets. Assess confidence in hitting quarterly targets.
- **Monthly focus:** Identify the top one or two focus areas for the coming month.
- **Bet review:** Align on the Bets that will have the most impact on delivering the monthly focus and should be prioritised
- **Issues & asks:** Identify support needed or blockers to resolve.

**Preparation**

Updated metrics and status, progress on previous focus areas and bets, recommendation for next month’s focus and bets.

**Attendees**

Strategy unit Squad PMs / Leads, Product Area leadership, others as relevant.

**Logistics**

First Monday and Tuesday of each month. ~2 hours. Strategy unit shares outputs within 48 hours.


### Pre-read structure


General

- Provide an update on the key metrics you are tracking
- Anything you want to discuss specifically
    

OKRs update

- Why: Keep squads grounded in the larger focus that their squad is contributing to
- What: What is the current status of the OKR that your squad is aligned to. How has it moved over recent months
- Learning: What have you learned in the past month from experiments and initiatives that is informing future work
    

Review last month's progress

- Update on progress of top priority from last month
- Did you complete what was agreed last month and what was the learning. If you didn't complete, are there lessons you can take forward to this month to be more effective? Will you approach you work differently this month as a result?
- What other things of note did you complete and what did you learn

Plans for next month

- Which objective will you focus on this month
- Which KR(s). Which one or maybe two KRs is the priority for the upcoming month
- Top priorities. What are your top priorities. Problem spaces, not task lists
- Experiments or initiatives. What are the experiments or initiatives that you plan to deliver this month to drive the top priority
    

Blockers & asks

- What things are blocking or delaying your work
- Where are you dependent on another team and what are you doing about it
- What specific asks do you have of leadership
