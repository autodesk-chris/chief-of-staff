
**Overview**
- **Purpose**: Provide the opportunity for squads and leadership to examine progress and decide what to do next.
- **Desired Outcome**: Clear monthly focus, confidence in quarterly targets, and early identification of issues and support needed.


**Outline**

Metrics Update
- Metric overview
	- OKR 
		- Confidence - on track, at risk, likely failure
		- Commentary. Include whether they have moved
		- Blockers & asks
		- Short and succinct for easy ready
	- Other metrics tracking
		- Are they heading in the right direction 
		- Show progress over time
		- Commentary
	- Experiment velocity
		- How many experiments went live in the last month
		- How many experiments completed in the past month


 Review Last Month's Progress
 - Learning & user insights
	  - What have you learned in the past month from experiments and initiatives and how is this learning informing what you do next?
	 
 Backlog (bets) review
- Updated backlog in priority order
- Summary of the bets focused on for this month
- Identify Initiatives vs experiments

 Next Month
 - Strategic Focus
	 - Which problem(s) will you focus on this month (not a task list)?
	  - What KR does it align to
	  - What metrics(s) will you focus on moving
- Bets & Initiatives review
	- What are your top (priority) bets for the month
		- Include a one or two sentence summary
		- Include link to initiative or experiment doc (these should be complete). Don't need to see all experiments, but ideally we'd have discussed those that will be your priorities for the month
		  - Expected sizing / effort

 Blockers, dependencies & Asks
 - Blockers
	  - What things are blocking or delaying your work?
	  - What are you doing to unblock them?

  - Dependencies
	  - Where are you dependent on another team?
	  - What's your plan to manage that dependency?

  - Asks
	- What specific asks do you have of leadership?


Action for teams:
- Define squad OKRs for this quarter
	Should be aligned to the leadership KR they are aligned to. 
	Can add other OKRs as relevant

  **Facilitator Notes**

  

  **Key Principles to Maintain:**

  - Focus on outcomes over outputs (user behavior changes, not shipped features)

  - Keep discussion data-informed (refer to metrics, evidence)

  - Surface ambiguity early (deliberate collaboration when boundaries blur)

  - Escalate decisions that cross squad/strategy/leadership boundaries

  

  **Red Flags to Watch For:**

  - Confidence dropping on quarterly targets without clear plan to recover

  - Same blockers appearing month over month without resolution

  - Vague priorities or too many priorities (lack of focus)

  - Bets that aren't linked to specific KRs

  - Outputs being discussed instead of outcomes

  

  **Follow-Up Requirements:**

  - Strategy unit shares outputs within 48 hours

  - Clear documentation of monthly focus and committed bets

  - Action items on asks and blockers assigned with owners
