## Executive summary

**Overall grade: B**

A well-structured pre-read with honest metrics reporting and clear experiment tracking. The squad is confronting a serious KR1 regression (0.9% vs 2.3% baseline) without papering over it. The Snowflake blocker that stalled the primary lever for 3+ months is finally unblocked, which is significant. However, the document lacks urgency given the scale of the gaps - 6 of 9 measurable KRs are red or behind track at the halfway point, and the plan for May is largely "continue what we started" rather than forcing a step-change. The GeT experiment produced a genuinely important learning (external access model doesn't work; in-product delivery needed) but the document doesn't push hard enough on what that means for the approach.

**Key strengths:**
- Honest about KR1 regression and explicit that drivers aren't fully understood yet
- GeT experiment insight is high-quality: 93% open rate proves timing is solved, 86% non-engagement proves the channel is wrong
- Support metrics held through a complex multi-product launch - operational execution is solid
- Clean experiment-to-learning-to-next-step connections throughout

**Key weaknesses:**
- KR1 dropped from 2.3% baseline to 0.9% - below baseline at the halfway point with no clear explanation yet
- 6 of 9 measurable KRs at risk or behind track; the plan doesn't address whether targets need adjustment
- No capacity allocation across initiatives - how much of the squad is on Trig vs support vs insights?
- Feedback Triage deprioritised for the second consecutive month without a clear re-entry plan

## Strategic issues and gaps

**Issue 1: Multiple KRs below baseline at the halfway point - no feasibility discussion**
- KR1: 0.9% vs 2.3% baseline (going backward), target 5%
- KR2: 0.3% vs 1.4% baseline (going backward), target 5%
- Obj2 KR2: 9.4% vs 13% baseline (going backward), target 30%
- Obj2 KR3: 35.2% vs 43% baseline (going backward), target 60%
- The document doesn't address whether these targets remain achievable. Four KRs moving in the wrong direction at the halfway point needs an honest conversation about what's realistic.

**Issue 2: Board first strike is invisible**
- Board KR2 dropped from 1.4% to 0.3% - the steepest decline of any KR - and receives zero attention in the May plan. No initiative, no experiment, no discussion.
- If Board is consciously deprioritised, it should say so explicitly. If it's expected to improve as a byproduct of Trig, that hypothesis needs stating.

**Issue 3: All bets are on Trig**
- Trig was blocked for 3+ months. It's now live. The entire May plan for activation and re-engagement rests on Trig lifecycle scaling.
- What's the fallback if Trig doesn't move the numbers? The squad has no alternative lever identified.
- The document needs a "if Trig shows X by end of May, we'll do Y; if not, Z" decision framework.

**Issue 4: The post-launch cohort mix question is unresolved**
- KR1 dropped from ~2.5% to 0.9%. The document says "the post-surge cohort mix has changed significantly" but "exact drivers are not yet fully understood."
- If the denominator changed structurally (ACC users flooding in who aren't suited to SD workflows), then the KR definition or denominator needs addressing. This was flagged in the April review too.

**Issue 5: Capacity allocation invisible**
- 2 priorities, 4 initiatives, 6 AI experiments, plus ongoing support operations. No indication of how squad capacity is split across these.

## Critical questions for the meeting

**Must ask:**

1. **"KR1 and KR2 are both below baseline at month 3. Board is at 0.3% vs a 5% target with no plan to address it. Should we adjust targets, change approach, or explicitly deprioritise Board?"** - Forces the honest conversation about what's achievable and what trade-offs are being made.

2. **"What's the hypothesis for how Trig closes a 5.6x gap on KR1 in 4 months? What would you expect to see by end of May to confirm the approach is working?"** - Tests whether there's a credible path or whether this is hope-based planning.

3. **"Four KRs are moving in the wrong direction. Is this primarily a cohort mix problem (ACC users in denominator) or a genuine activation problem? Because the answer changes the strategy entirely."** - The most important diagnostic question. If it's denominator, fix the metric definition. If it's activation, Trig alone may not be enough.

4. **"The GeT experiment proved external learning doesn't work. What's the concrete plan for in-product learning at moment of need? Is this a squad deliverable or a product dependency?"** - The insight is great but the "next hypothesis" needs to become a real initiative with owners and timeline.

5. **"What percentage of squad capacity is on Trig vs support vs insights vs FCC prep? Are you resourced for the priorities you've set?"** - Tests capacity reality.

**If time:**

6. **"Feedback Triage has been deprioritised for two months. Is it dropped or coming back? If coming back, when?"** - Prevents silent drop of a committed initiative.

7. **"The Trig/Mixpanel data alignment risk - what's the timeline to resolve this? If KR numbers aren't comparable across systems, how are you reporting confidence?"** - Measurement integrity question.
