## 5. Strategic issues and gaps

**Issue 1: The 60% meaningful action target is not credible**
- 4.3% actual vs 60% target = 14x gap with 4 months remaining
- The document notes this but frames it as "either the target needs rebaselining or the meaningful action set must expand significantly"
- Even with a broader definition, closing a 14x gap in 4 months is not feasible. This needs a rebaselining decision now - carrying it forward as-is damages reporting credibility.

**Issue 2: The CURR gate makes KR3/KR4 unachievable as written**
- Zero accounts at the 75% gate. Org-wide CURR is ~58%. Asking individual accounts to be 17pp above the org average is structurally implausible for most.
- Anders proposes three paths but doesn't recommend one. The meeting needs to make this decision.
- **Recommendation:** Path 1 (recalibrate to 60-65%) combined with Path 2 (CURR as Playbook KPI) is the pragmatic answer.

**Issue 3: Capacity invisible across 8 high-priority items**
- The backlog lists: Playbook MVP, Sales & Adoption Strategy, KR baseline lock, Signal-Action Routing, AI infrastructure, BD First Strike definition, CS leadership commitment, and TBD owner claims
- No effort estimates, no sequencing, no capacity allocation
- This is Gap 5 from the framework (Capacity Allocation Invisible)

**Issue 4: Analytics baseline lock - chronic dependency (3 months)**
- Same blocker, same management plan, same slip. The approach isn't working.
- Needs escalation - not another "sync with Craig"

**Issue 5: Signal-Action Routing experiment is in limbo**
- DEVELOPMENT since March 18. Dependent on the same Analytics blocker. Either commit to a pilot with whatever data exists, or formally pause and remove from high priority.
