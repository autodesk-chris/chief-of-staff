# MFM review: First Strike - May/June 2026

## Executive summary

**Grade: C+**

The squad delivered on experiments and maintained cadence despite capacity constraints and the Oslo offsite. However, every KR is trending in the wrong direction - KR2 has collapsed from ~40% to 7.91%, meaningful actions and retention metrics are declining, and the document does not confront whether current targets are achievable. The pre-read is honest about the numbers but lacks root cause analysis for the declines and doesn't present a credible path to closing massive gaps. The June plan is reasonable in structure but the absence of math connecting planned work to target recovery is the biggest gap.

Note: This is a combined April + partial May edition covering Apr 1 - May 19.

## Dimensional analysis

### 1. Examine progress
**Score: Adequate**
- Numbers are presented honestly with month-over-month trends
- Good experiment documentation with clear hypotheses, outcomes, and learnings
- However: KR2 collapse from ~40% to 7.91% lacks root cause analysis in the main text (flagged for manual attention but not explained)
- The March-to-May decline in meaningful actions (56% to 38.6%) is noted but not diagnosed
- NURR reversal after 3 months of improvement not investigated

### 2. Strategic clarity
**Score: Adequate**
- Three clear experiment series with capacity allocation percentages (30/20/50) is good
- Explicit deprioritization of gradual disclosure with rationale (Tyto risks) shows trade-off thinking
- However: the shift to 50% US feature delivery is a major strategic pivot not fully justified by the data presented
- Several "Draft" and "Next: check with..." items suggest plans are still forming

### 3. Monthly focus
**Score: Needs work**
- Problem statement is clear (Activation / First Session Value Gap)
- But focus is spread across onboarding, profiling, US features, desktop access, homepage simplification, Tyto discovery, Unified Home, and multiple strategic initiatives
- Hard to state in 1-2 sentences what June's focus is and why it will work
- Significant capacity on non-KR discovery work (Tyto, Unified Home, Unified Profiles) without clear justification

### 4. Target confidence
**Score: Needs work**
- Every KR is at "Challenge" status with enormous gaps (22% vs 70%, 7.9% vs 60%, 10.6% vs 50%)
- No discussion of whether targets are achievable
- No math showing path from current state to target
- No options presented (adjust targets? accept partial achievement? what would need to fundamentally change?)
- The document proceeds as if the targets are still the plan despite being further from them than baseline

### 5. Issue identification
**Score: Adequate**
- Blockers are identified with mitigation approaches
- Dependencies (ACC Hub, Scenarios) are named
- Asks are specific and actionable (Tyto clarity, Unified Home director attention)
- However: the team size vulnerability blocker has been present for months without structural resolution
- Missing: the KR2 collapse is the biggest issue and it's flagged only in a manual-attention appendix, not in the main blockers section

## Key questions to ask

1. **KR2 has collapsed from ~40% (March) to 7.91%. What caused this?** The Automated Onboarding experiment ending is likely the main driver, but this isn't diagnosed. Understanding this is critical - it determines whether the baseline has permanently shifted and what the real starting point is for future experiments.

2. **Given the gap sizes (47pp, 52pp, 39pp to targets), are the H1 KR targets still meaningful planning tools?** The document doesn't address feasibility. At current trajectory, none are achievable. Should the team propose revised targets, or reframe as "maximize rate of improvement"?

3. **The shift to 50% capacity on US feature delivery - what's the expected impact on KR metrics?** This is the biggest capacity allocation but has the least defined expected outcomes. How does Cost & ROI, shadow study, etc. translate to KR movement?

4. **What's the actual status of Smart Site Setup V1?** Page says Live but inner status says "FORMING". This was a 50/50 A/B test targeting KR2 and WF1. Are there results? Why is it paused?

5. **Tyto and Unified Home are consuming discovery capacity and driving deprioritization (gradual disclosure, AI onboarding). What's the expected timeline for clarity?** The squad is reacting to these org-level initiatives but can't control their pace. What's the plan if clarity takes another 2-3 months?

6. **The Desktop Access Point email showed the shortcut works (+5pp) but only 9.7% install. What's the next move?** The learning is clear (install conversion is the lever, not the email). Is the "Home page Install Surface" experiment the follow-up, and what's the expected install rate improvement?

**If time:**

7. Intercom impact measuring has been carried forward for 3+ months. Should it be explicitly dropped?
8. The onboarding series is being closed early to "rebuild from learnings" - what are the specific learnings being carried into the new experience?
