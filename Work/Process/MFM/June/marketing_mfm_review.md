## 2. Topics for the meeting

### 1. Ben's 3-4 priority themes - what drives Marketing success?

- What are the 3-4 priority themes that most things in Marketing should be about? (e.g., Messaging, GTM as a channel, AI productivity, Demand gen / lead quality - Ben to define.)
- Once we have the themes, force the June bets to map to them. Anything that doesn't, drops or defers.
- US is your #1 4Ps priority. How does the US tilt show up in the themes, not just in AIA execution?
- Expect to redraft the bet list together against the themes.

### 2. Messaging strategy

Surfaced in April, still not closed. Site Design messaging sprints listed for June; the wider arc is missing.

- Where's the cross-product Marketing messaging strategy (not per-launch)?
- BD IUP messaging testing (SketchDeck) silently dropped from the June pre-read. What happened?

### 3. GTM as a channel beyond Building Design

A real gap. Forma needs a GTM strategy that isn't a launch-by-launch motion.

- What does GTM as a channel look like beyond the BD launch model?
- Street Design GTM is in the June list but reads as early scoping. Want a more deliberate view.
- ATN sponsorship + Pecha Kucha repeatable format: what's the plan? Sponsoring ATN 2027? Pecha Kucha as a quarterly motion?

### 4. Metrics - lead quality, Marketing-generated content, BD KR

Three linked gaps.

- How are leads being counted? What does quality mean? How is it measured today? (3,210 in the rolling window is volume; quality signal is missing.)
- Want metrics on Marketing-generated content (PMM-led, paid, owned) as a distinct view from Community-generated content. Volume, reach, conversion attribution.
- BD KR update - April promised a new metric (PC visits + conversion actions). Where did that land?

### 5. FCC launch + AI help

Two short asks.

- FCC launch was discussed in April, absent in June. Status and next checkpoint?
- On the AI agent build-out (5+ shipped): what help does the team need? Tooling access (DAM, winget) is one part. Patterns, people, leadership air cover?

### 6. AIA - what does success look like?

The plan is dense; the success definition isn't.

- Define success now: AIA+14 and AIA+30 scorecards. Leads captured, MAU conversion, owner.
- "15% MAU within 1 month" - where does that come from? Whose plan is it?
