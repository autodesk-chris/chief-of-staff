## What to push on in the meeting (your priorities)

### Specific items to raise

1. Walk me through engagement depth. Present the meaningful-actions-per-active-user table and tell me what you're doing about each row. Specifically: what is the play on Stantec this week (creation -81%, #1 by MAU)? What changed at Arup (+222%) and SSOE (+188%) and how are you porting it? What is Jacobs (-49%) telling us? This is the most important page of the document and the action plan against it should not be implicit.
2. Engagement depth is Site Design only today (Add / Edit / Run / Order / Invite). What is each account's actual use of Building Design and Board? Can we track it at the same per-account granularity, and if not, what is the path to instrument it? Without this, the scorecard sees one product surface, not the customer's reality - and the BD enablement gap (SWECO token surprise) stays invisible until it surfaces in a meeting.
3. Send the Forma Playbook MVP link - want to read it.
4. Sales & Adoption strategy: next steps now that it's published? What's the leadership distribution plan and what action does it drive?
5. Sales enablement hire: where is it on contract? Belongs in the Progress section, not just under June asks. What's the date if it's the gating constraint for Sub-strategy 1?
6. Sales team support in June: James mentioned training and an offsite. Is the Boston offsite (June 30 / July 1) confirmed? What's the contingency if dates slip?
7. WWS enablement in June: 'first structured session with a partner' is thin. What is the outcome of that session - what should the partner be able to do after it that they can't today? Frame as outcome.
8. AI strategy: what is the one experiment in June that will have material impact, and why? 'Skills mapped' is not an outcome. Reframe as one shareable AI capability shipped to the squad with a measurable use case.
9. AI sub-strategy June focus: one sentence test - what is the one thing you are trying to prove in June?
10. Building Design First Strike: leads have agreement on the definition; Squads need to finalise. Is this a blocker or a process step? What timing should I push for from Squad leadership?
11. CSM capacity: the doc says ~10 accounts already stretches a CSM. I thought we were at 2. What changed in the count, and what needs to change (remit, hire, scope cut) to enable the planned growth?
12. Scorecard as a published artefact: could this be an agent? Need to publish at least some scorecards to make them visible - sales accounts + strategic-account-managed ones. Each account needs a target on the chosen metric, a problem statement (so we diagnose and focus on the right things), and a clear execution plan.

### How to use these in the room

Lead with the outcomes reframe (point above). Then walk through the two double-downs and ask Anders to write back the June outcomes in that frame. Items 1-10 are the prompts to use as you go.
