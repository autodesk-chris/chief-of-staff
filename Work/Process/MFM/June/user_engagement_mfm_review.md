## 2. Five topics for this meeting

### Topic 1. Trig: from first strike to account-level lifecycle - is the build plan the right one?

The pivot is significant: June's bets are stages 1-4 (org setup, activation, multiple-user activation, feature depth) for small and medium orgs only. This is a bet that org-level intervention beats individual nudges. It is also the right shape for your one-to-one monitoring of all users ambition.

- Test the prioritisation: is "stage 5 - continued usage" really the right "if time" item, or is that where the lever actually sits for KR3 (current users 2+ days/month, 49.2% vs 65% target)?
- Capacity reality check: stages 1-4 plus a backlog of four "if time" jobs in one month, on top of Trig Agent operating Arne solo, looks ambitious. Force a ranked top 2.
- Large enterprise stages are explicitly deferred to July (decision already made). Confirm that is still the right call given the new emphasis on small and medium orgs.
- Get a measurement plan: with traffic capped and no statistically significant lift after a month, what does Arne need to prove incremental impact before September? Is it more traffic, more time, or a different test design?

### Topic 2. In-product learning - why parked, and what replaces it?

This is the most important silent shift in the doc. The May 12 meeting agreed that the next experiment after GeT was in-product learning delivery at the moment of need. June parks that experiment and replaces it with "modular learning content library" planning only.

- Why parked? Capacity, learning, or a real change of strategy?
- If the conclusion from GeT is that learning needs to happen in-product, parking the in-product test removes the only active hypothesis for OBJ3.
- The replacement is a content library - that is supply, not demand. Where is the demand-side experiment?
- Planning, not delivery, in June is a thin commitment for a quarter-on-track KR. Push Malak (via Joe) on what minimum viable delivery looks like before end of June.

### Topic 3. KR2 Board first strike - 0.4% against a 5% target

KR1 (Site Design) is the headline recovery story. KR2 (Board) is the one nobody is talking about: 0.4% current vs 5% target with three months to September. The June plan has nothing for Board because BD FSM is undefined.

- This is a feasibility conversation, not a tactics conversation. With current trajectory, this KR will land closer to 1% than 5%.
- What is the squad's actual plan for KR2 - or should it be honestly reframed as H2?
- BD FSM blocker has been live for two months. What unblock action is Joe willing to commit to this month (who he is escalating to, by when)? If "no response from BD squad" is the only answer, that is an issue for you to drive, not him.

### Topic 4. Building Design FSM and FCC dependencies - whose problem is this?

Two of the squad's biggest blockers (BD Trig stages, FCC content) come down to other squads not having locked their FSM or workflow definitions.

- Confirm explicit owners on the Forma Design side: who is chasing the BD squad on FSM? Who is chasing Thao/Carl via Daria for the FCC walkthrough recording?
- This is exactly the kind of cross-squad dependency that the operating model is supposed to surface monthly. If it has been raised twice without resolution, escalate.
- Linked to your wider Forma Design / Forma Construction contribution model work from last week - is BD FSM something that contribution model conversation could resolve?

### Topic 5. Autodesk Assistant / Fin resourcing - the only formal ask, and it is yours

The only ask of leadership is the Autodesk Assistant resourcing. Joe also still needs to co-author the long-term support business case with you for Carl.

- Confirm what Joe needs from you specifically: a named technical resource, an Ecosystem squad commitment, or a business case decision first?
- Get a date on the Carl business case. Drift here is what is keeping the squad in monthly-ask mode rather than enabling Joe to drive.
