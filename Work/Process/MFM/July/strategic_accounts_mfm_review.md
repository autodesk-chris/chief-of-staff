## 2. Six topics for this meeting

### Topic 1. Robin's sales training programme - tell me more

The June commitment landed on paper. Now understand what's real.
- What is the "trained" bar in practice - what does a rep do to prove they've cleared it?
- How is the call-recording loop actually running? Whose calls, who's reviewing, what's the cadence?
- The learning from June was that the gap is strategic/account-management, not demo tech. What has that changed in the plan since?
- Enablement contractor pending signature - what does month one look like, and what do they own vs Robin?
- Toronto Day 2 (Jul 29) is the first full-team test at 3-persona depth. What's the plan B if the SME content doesn't land by Jul 17? (pre-read fallback is AutoCAD-only)

### Topic 2. Scorecards + Forma Design Coach - where are we, how are we leveraging

The most concrete agentic delivery in Growth right now. Use this to learn.
- Where are the scorecards up to - 6 scored + \~18 batchable is the number. But how are they actually being used? Who reads them, when does a play fire, who acts?
- Escalation-with-data on the first \~20 accounts - what does that loop look like end to end? First example?
- Forma Design Coach GPT: what does it do in one sentence? Who's using it and for what? Is there a signal that it's actually shifting behaviour vs a nice-to-have?
- Post-call coaching cycle - how does a transcript flow through to a playbook update? What's the human step vs the automated step?
- Squad memory: what's the operating pattern - what gets captured, who consumes it?

### Topic 3. Playbook front door - MindTickle decision

The pivot from SharePoint to MindTickle is presented as decided but worth pressure-testing.
- Why MindTickle specifically? What does it give you that SharePoint didn't?
- What does the contractor actually own once they're in?
- What's the migration plan, and what does "live with first content populated" mean by end of July?
- Risk check: is this a real solve, or moving the front-door problem sideways?

### Topic 4. Worldwide Sales - platinum partner plan + user feedback + Arup/SSOE

Combined because Sid has one slot's worth of substance and one open push.
- Platinum partner plan: drifted in May, drifted in June. What's the actual concrete plan now - owner, success criteria, account matrix, dates? Not "in progress."
- User feedback compiled with Clement and delivered to leadership - what are the top 2-3 signals? What is it telling us to change in the playbook or the product? Anything customer-side you want me to carry?
- Arup and SSOE were the June "learn from" pattern. July scorecard reclassifies both as borderline. What did we actually learn - and what needs to come out of the playbook, not go in? Model the honesty publicly.

### Topic 5. KR4 - population problem or framing problem?

Anders flags this as likely failure. But the question is deeper than "hit or miss."
- 100-199 band has 3 accounts, 1 healthy. Target of 6 isn't reachable on the current account set.
- Is that unreachable, or is the KR framed against the wrong denominator? Are there accounts sitting outside the roster that should be counted - accounts growing INTO the band from sub-100?
- Could a re-framed KR4 measure direction-of-travel across a wider set (sub-100 → 100-199, or 100-199 → 200+)? That would reward the growth pattern we actually want, not just the state.
- Land a decision: hold at 6 accepting the miss, re-scope target, or re-scope account set. Don't leave this one drifting into August.

### Topic 6. Forma Sales team - initial accounts + expectations with Ken/James

This is the topic that connects most to your #1 4Ps problem and to a follow-up you'll take outside the room.
- "First 2-3 hands-on accounts as playbook input" - which accounts, what does the test look like, what's the exit criteria for the squad handing off?
- Where does the squad stop and the Forma sales team start once they're trained? Specifically on a live account: who owns the discovery call, who owns the value narrative, who owns escalation?
- Reconcile the ownership read: pre-read says "confirmed with Ken/James, squad enables." Your 4Ps says Ken has rejected Sales ownership. Ask Anders directly what "confirmed" actually means.
- Signal to raise: there's a version of this that's a broader pivot away from Selling. Worth picking up separately with Ken + Arif to understand the strategy team's read. Not for this meeting to resolve, but flag to Anders that you're taking that conversation.

### Topic 7 (if time). Analysis adoption - understanding value, not just first strike

The pre-read's honest headline: WSP 90/96 Site Design users never analysed, Jacobs 103/108, Stantec 583 MAU / 1 editor.
- We need to understand HOW users actually use the product, not just whether they hit first strike. What are the day-to-day users doing? Are they getting value some other way?
- Is First Strike the right lens or the wrong lens? If it's right, what's the concrete July play to move it? If it's wrong, what should we be measuring instead?
- Would a "usage map" (login vs open vs create vs analyse vs collaborate, by frequency, by persona) tell us something first strike hides? Who owns building that?
- Arcadis is the anomaly (depth +58%, Run +416%, broad base). What's actually different about how Arcadis users are using the product?
