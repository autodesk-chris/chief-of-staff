# MFM Review: First Strike - April 2026

## Executive summary

**Overall grade: C+**

The squad has a strong experimentation culture with impressive results (Automated Onboarding V2 delivered ~100% lift in FSM), and retention metrics (NURR, CURR) are trending positively. However, the document suffers from three significant weaknesses: (1) all four H1 KRs are at "Challenge" with no feasibility discussion or path-to-target math, (2) focus is spread across 8+ experiments, 7 always-on initiatives, 3 restarted initiatives, and a critical C-Suite ask - making it impossible to identify the top 2-3 priorities, and (3) a recurring instrumentation gap ("impact of new entry points needs to be instrumented") appears against every KR without a clear plan to resolve it.

## Key questions to ask

### Priority and capacity
1. **If you could only commit to 3 experiments this month, which 3 and why?** The document lists 8 experiments across 3 series. This tests whether real prioritisation has happened.
2. **What percentage of the squad's time goes to always-on initiatives vs strategic experiments?** With 7 always-on items plus 3 restarts, how much capacity is actually available for the experiment series?
3. **The C-Suite ask is marked Critical - what's the scope and resource commitment?** Could this consume significant capacity that's currently allocated to experiments?

### Measurement and targets
4. **When will the new entry point instrumentation be in place?** Every KR is flagged with this gap. Without it, how will you know if April's experiments are working?
5. **What's the current baseline for each H1 KR, and what's the math to reach target?** KR1 is 70% site entry - what is it today, and which experiments move it by how many points?
6. **H2 missed significantly (2.8% vs 10% on KR1). What's fundamentally different about H1 that makes equally ambitious targets achievable?**
7. **The shift from FSM to WF1 - is this a strategic pivot or a target adjustment?** What does this mean for how we measure squad success?

### Experiments and learning
8. **Automated Onboarding V2 delivered 100% FSM lift - why isn't scaling this the #1 priority?** It's the strongest result but appears as just one of many experiments.
9. **Three experiments are live and awaiting results (Self-Select Tutorial, Nudge to Board, Smart Site Setup). When do you expect readouts?** Given the 4-week data gathering constraint, these could still be in-flight through April.
10. **The ACC user research surfaced that Forma is invisible and ACC is wrong conversion surface. How does this change your entry rate strategy?** This seems like a fundamental insight that should reshape prioritisation.

### Dependencies and blockers
11. **Global Launch is April 7/9 - what's the expected impact on your metrics?** Building Design and Scenarios launching could significantly shift user behaviour and experiment validity.
12. **The data gathering speed blocker has been flagged before. What would structurally change this?** More users? Different experiment methodology? Different metrics?
13. **Intercom impact measuring and cross-squad learnings were both deprioritised last month and are "restarting." What evidence suggests they'll progress this month?**

### Strategy
14. **The vision document and FY27 strategy were drafted in March. What decisions need to be made in the MFM about strategic direction?** The document mentions three focus areas but doesn't ask for alignment or decisions.
15. **How does the "Scaled User Success" operating model question from User Engagement (Joseph's MFM) relate to your squad's strategy?** Both squads seem to be thinking about broader positioning.

## Strengths to acknowledge

- **Experimentation velocity and culture:** The squad maintains a strong cadence of well-structured experiments despite capacity constraints. ExperimentLab is a genuine innovation.
- **User research integration:** ACC-Forma research findings are high quality and directly feeding into experiment design. The five structural patterns are actionable insights.
- **Retention trend:** NURR increasing for three consecutive months is a meaningful signal that underlying product improvements are sticking.
- **Automated Onboarding V2:** A 100% lift in FSM is an exceptional result that validates the hypothesis about reducing friction to value.
- **Adaptability:** Defining US-specific FSM (WF1) shows responsiveness to market differences rather than forcing a one-size-fits-all metric.
