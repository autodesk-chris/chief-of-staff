# MFM Review: Strategic Accounts - April 2026

## Executive summary

**Overall grade: B-**

This is a well-structured, detailed pre-read that demonstrates strong strategic thinking and impressive KR4 execution. However, it has a fundamental problem: two months into H1, three of four KRs have no actuals because Analytics baselines haven't been confirmed. The squad is doing excellent account-level work (KR4 exceeded target) and has built impressive foundational assets (Playbook Map v1, taxonomy, demo assets), but the inability to measure KR1-3 means the squad is flying blind on 75% of its objectives. The Sales Workshop (April 20-21) is correctly identified as the highest-leverage item, but the document doesn't adequately address what happens if baselines remain unconfirmed - there's no escalation plan beyond "requesting this be prioritized."

## Key questions to ask

### Priority and focus (most critical)

1. **"It's month 2 of 6 and we can't measure 3 of 4 KRs. What's the escalation plan if Analytics doesn't deliver baselines by mid-April?"** This isn't a request anymore - it's a KR-threatening blocker. What does Anders need from you to unblock this?

2. **"After the workshop on April 20-21, how many enablement sessions can you realistically run before June? Is that enough data points to evaluate KR3?"** The math matters here. If you need N sessions for a meaningful rating and can only fit M, better to know now.

3. **"You have six bets for April with a global launch in week 1 and a workshop in week 3. What's the capacity plan? What gets deprioritized if something slips?"** The document lists bets but doesn't show how the team's time is allocated across them.

### Strategic direction

4. **"KR4 is exceeded with 3 months left. Should the target move up, or should focus shift from account volume to usage depth?"** The squad correctly flags this question in the learnings. Push for a decision.

5. **"Three accounts dropped out of the top 12 (Perkins+Will, HKS, Shive-Hattery). Are they missing the same deliberate interventions driving the top performers?"** Good diagnostic question already raised in the document - make sure it gets answered, not just noted.

6. **"The Compare tool has ~70% NURR but only 6% adoption. That's the biggest single lever in the document. Is there a dedicated push on this beyond the launch?"** This data point deserves its own bet, not a mention inside the launch bet.

### Operating model

7. **"What happens if the workshop doesn't produce a clear RACI? What's the fallback?"** The document positions the workshop as the single dependency for everything downstream. That's high-stakes for a 1.5-day event.

8. **"The operating model has been undefined for 2 months. What's been the cost of that ambiguity so far? Any specific examples of work that stalled?"** Understanding the cost makes the case for why workshop outputs must be binding, not advisory.

### Execution and learning

9. **"The Playbook thesis is compelling - deliberate interventions drive growth. Can you rank the 10 undocumented plays by expected impact? Which 3 would move KR1/KR2 most?"** Five plays in April is ambitious. Better to nail 3 high-impact ones than partially document 5.

10. **"The contractor hire for enablement delivery - where is that in the process? When would they realistically start?"** This was flagged as critical capacity relief but there's no timeline update.

## Strengths to acknowledge

- **KR4 execution is outstanding.** Nine accounts at 100+ MAUs vs target of 6, with clear explanations of what drove growth at each account. This is what good strategic accounts work looks like.
- **The Playbook Map v1 is genuine strategic infrastructure.** 12 problem/outcome pairs, gap analysis, retention signal integration - this creates the foundation for scalable execution. The sequencing decision (codify before delivering) was brave and correct.
- **Learning quality is exceptional.** 12 learnings, each with a clear "so what" and connection to next actions. The Arcadis upskilling validation, shadow study regulatory insight, and BAM construction-phase discovery are all high-value signals.
- **Honesty throughout.** Red ratings on 3 of 4 KRs, clear identification of systemic Product dependency, acknowledgment that the operating model is undefined. This is a team that knows where it stands.

## Recommendations for the meeting

**Start with:** The measurement crisis. 2 months in, 3 of 4 KRs unmeasurable. This needs a decision, not another request. What concrete escalation will unlock Analytics baselines this month?

**Push on:** Capacity allocation for April. Six bets + global launch + workshop is a lot. Ask Anders to name the top 3 if he had to cut the list in half. Also push on KR3 math - is there enough runway for meaningful measurement?

**Acknowledge:** KR4 performance and Playbook foundation work. This squad has done genuinely impressive strategic work. Make sure they feel that recognition alongside the hard questions about measurement.

**Don't leave without:** (1) An agreed escalation path for Analytics baselines with a deadline, (2) A decision on whether KR4 target revises upward or focus shifts to depth, (3) Clarity on what "success" looks like for the workshop - what specific outputs must emerge.
